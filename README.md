# Tycho Collins

Co-Founder, Vestigia Labs · New York, NY

I build AI that does real research work in finance. Vestigia is an AI research
terminal for investors in Chinese stocks. It monitors news, filings, and
regulators, runs on its own schedule, and every number it reports traces back
to a source document. An ETF manager is a paying client.

## What I work on

- **Autonomous research workflows:** AI that reads filings and news and hands
  analysts finished work they can check against the source
- **Full stack:** Python, Flutter, PostgreSQL, Google Cloud, AWS
- **Business side:** client relationships, pricing, and partnerships, alongside
  my co-founders
